# 1. The Universe

In a vast universe filled with different civilizations, one highly advanced civilization has reached a level of
technological development beyond the understanding of most other species.

With almost unlimited resources and power, its people have grown bored of their existence. Seeking entertainment,
they begin interfering with less advanced civilizations, treating entire planets and their inhabitants as pieces in
a game.

They create dangerous trials, manipulate conflicts, and force civilizations to fight for survival. To them, the
destruction of a planet is nothing more than entertainment.

Some civilizations are promised salvation if they successfully complete these trials. Those who fail risk having
their entire planets destroyed or harvested for resources.

This civilization also controls several subordinate organizations and experimental facilities, which carry out its
activities across the universe.

However, not everyone is willing to accept this cruelty.

A resistance and rescue organization, known as **Earth Vanguard** (temporary name), has emerged to protect
endangered civilizations and fight against the influence of this advanced civilization.
