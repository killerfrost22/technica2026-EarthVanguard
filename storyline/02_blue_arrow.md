# 2. The Main Character: The Escaped Experiment

The main protagonist is a young woman, **Blue Arrow**, who was once a test subject in an experimental facility
controlled by a subordinate organization of the advanced civilization. Inside, she had no name, only a number:
Subject 7.

She spent much of her life confined within the facility, isolated from the outside world and subjected to
experiments.

## Echo: her escape

Her escape is the game **Echo**.

In Echo, the player controls Blue Arrow, a girl separated from society and trapped in a dark and unfamiliar
environment. Unable to see the world around her, she must rely on sound waves to reveal hidden paths and navigate
through dangerous obstacles.

The darkness and obstacles are the experimental facility and the challenges she faced while escaping. The experiments
left her one gift, the ability to see with sound, and it is what gets her out. Every echo that shows her the way can
also be heard by the guards.

Her climb toward the surface is her desire for freedom, belonging, and a place where she can finally feel safe.
Stepping out under the open sky is her successful escape from the facility.

## Afterward

After gaining her freedom, she eventually joins Earth Vanguard, determined to prevent others from experiencing the
same suffering. There she meets Puff, who becomes her best friend and fighting buddy.

Her past also gives her a personal reason to oppose the advanced civilization responsible for the experiments.
