# 2. The Main Character: The Escaped Experiment

The main protagonist is a young woman, **Blue Angel**, who was once a test subject in an experimental facility
controlled by a subordinate organization of the advanced civilization.

She spent much of her life confined within the facility, isolated from the outside world and subjected to
experiments.

## Echo: her escape

Her escape is represented through the game **Echo**.

In Echo, the player controls a girl separated from society, trapped in a dark and unfamiliar environment. Unable to
see the world around her, she must rely on sound waves to reveal hidden paths and navigate through dangerous
obstacles.

The fish represents the protagonist herself, while the darkness and obstacles symbolize the experimental facility
and the challenges she faced while escaping.

The fish's search for its school represents the protagonist's desire for freedom, belonging, and a place where she
can finally feel safe.

Reaching the school symbolizes her successful escape from the facility.

## Afterward

After gaining her freedom, she eventually joins Earth Vanguard, determined to prevent others from experiencing the
same suffering.

Her past also gives her a personal reason to oppose the advanced civilization responsible for the experiments.
