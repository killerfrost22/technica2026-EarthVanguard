# 3. Puff: The Survivor of a Lost Planet

**Puff** is a pufferfish from an ocean planet that was once filled with beautiful coral reefs and marine life.

Over time, his home became severely polluted by the evil corporations, leaving its oceans dark and filled with
debris.

The advanced civilization eventually selected his planet as the setting for one of its cruel games.

Puff was given an opportunity to save his home by completing a series of dangerous trials. He was promised the power
necessary to restore his planet if he succeeded.

Determined to save his people, Puff accepted the challenge.

## Puff's Ocean: the trials

These events are represented through the **Puff** minigame, where he navigates polluted waters, avoids deadly
obstacles, and struggles to complete the trials.

However, even after overcoming the challenges and the experiment, his planet was ultimately destroyed.

## Afterward

Puff survived, but he lost his home and the civilization he had fought so desperately to protect.

Knowing the truth about what happened, he eventually joined Earth Vanguard, hoping to prevent other worlds from
suffering the same fate. There he became Blue Arrow's best friend and fighting buddy.

Unlike the main protagonist, Puff remembers what happened to his home but is reluctant to discuss it.

His experiences are gradually revealed through conversations and memory sequences as Blue Arrow earns his
trust.
