## In one paragraph

A civilization so advanced it has grown bored turns other worlds into games: it stages deadly trials, promises
salvation to the winners, and destroys or harvests the losers. Blue Arrow escaped one of its experimental
facilities; Puff fought through its trials to save his ocean planet and lost it anyway. Now best friends and
fighting buddies, both fly for Earth Vanguard, a resistance and rescue organization. Two survivors who once struggled to save themselves now fight
to save others.
